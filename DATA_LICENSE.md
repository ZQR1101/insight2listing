The MIT license applies to project source code only. It does not grant rights to Amazon content, customer reviews, product images, brand names, logos, fonts, third-party datasets, or user-uploaded files.

The initial repository should use synthetic or clearly licensed example data. A future data connector must record its source, license, permitted use, observed time, and retention rules. Users are responsible for confirming that they have the right to upload and process data through a connector.
